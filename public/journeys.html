The Journeys

For seventeen years Coles followed the children of a changing nation across the map — from one New Orleans schoolhouse to the hollows of Appalachia, the projects of the North, the mesas of Arizona, the Arctic coast, and the mansions of the rich. Trace the route of Children of Crisis.

The interactive map needs a Mapbox token. Add your publishable token as the MAPBOX_TOKEN variable in wrangler.jsonc and redeploy. Meanwhile, the itinerary below lists every place on the route.

Each marker is a place Coles lived among the people he wrote about — colored by which strand of the work brought him there. The dashed arcs trace the The South Goes North migration of Volume III, as families left the rural South for the industrial cities. Select any stop to read what he found there, and to step into the matching book.
