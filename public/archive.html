The Archive

Coles wrote more than fifty books and well over a thousand articles, essays, and reviews. Much of it is now within reach — digitized to borrow, or preserved in the great manuscript collections. This page exists to send you to the work itself.

The manuscript papers

Where the originals live

Coles's personal and professional archives — correspondence, manuscripts, research files, and the field records behind his books — are held at two universities. They are the primary sources for any serious study of his work.

The periodicals

A thousand essays

Before they were books, many of Coles's ideas appeared as essays — most famously in The Atlantic Monthly, to which he contributed for decades and where his first articles on Ruby Bridges and the desegregating South appeared. He wrote, all told, well over a thousand pieces for periodicals and professional journals. The Atlantic's online archive is the best starting point for the magazine writing.
